Provenance and upstream terms
-----------------------------
These images were generated with StyleGAN2 (NVIDIA) using a model trained on the
Flickr-Faces-HQ (FFHQ) dataset. FFHQ is distributed by NVIDIA under
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and is intended for non-commercial research; the StyleGAN2
FFHQ-trained model weights are likewise released under a non-commercial license.
The non-commercial terms therefore flow through to these synthetic images, which
is why they cannot be released under a permissive or commercial license.

Credit:
  - StyleGAN2: T. Karras et al., NVIDIA. https://github.com/NVlabs/stylegan2
  - FFHQ dataset: NVIDIA. https://github.com/NVlabs/ffhq-dataset

The images are synthetic and are not intended to depict identifiable real
individuals.

Before public release, confirm that the specific StyleGAN2 model/weights used
permit redistribution of the selected generated images under these terms.
